Our Arcadia store sits on this corridor. AG-75, Ground Floor, Block-A, Unitech Arcadia, Sector 49 is a walk-in counter, not a call centre address. If you live or work along the Sectors 47 to 49 stretch, that shop is your nearest Wash4You, and it is the reason we can run pickups here without treating your flat as an outstation drop. You can hand over a suit at the counter on your way past, or never leave your flat and let the rider come to you. Both work, and both cost the same, because pickup and delivery are free.

The Sohna Road belt is mostly towers. Working couples, IT and consulting professionals, families with school-age kids, and a steady layer of tenants who move flats every couple of years. In most of these homes the daily washing already happens at home, which changes what actually leaves the building. What we collect here is the load a home machine and a bucket cannot fix: blazers and formal shirts for the office run, winter woollens and quilts at the end of the season, silk and embroidered ethnic wear before a function, curtains and fabric sofa covers that have gone grey without anyone noticing, and sneakers. A lot of sneakers. Suede and knit runners are where our before-and-after work is most visible, and it is the service this corridor asks for more than any other locality we serve.

Now the honest part about doorstep pickup in a condominium. The friction is never the road, it is the gate. A rider gets stopped at the visitor desk, the guard calls up, you are on a work call, and the whole thing turns into a fifteen-minute negotiation. So when you book on WhatsApp, send the tower and flat number along with the address, and mention if your society insists on riders waiting at the gate. Wherever the society allows it, we count the order with you at your door, not at the guard desk, and you get a written receipt before anything leaves your hands. Your order is then packed separately from every other customer's, which matters more than it sounds when a single tower sends us a dozen bags on the same evening. For the plotted houses and independent floors off the main corridor it is simpler: doorbell, count, receipt.

This is a price-aware area and we would rather you ask than guess. WhatsApp us the item, get the rate, then decide. Nazuk kapde, extra care, and nothing goes out unless it comes back the way you expected.
