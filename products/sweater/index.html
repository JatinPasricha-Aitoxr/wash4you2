    Knit Preservation

    Why Professional Dryclean Care for Sweaters?

    Knitted fabrics are loops of yarn, and heat plus agitation lock them together into felt. Here is why professional dryclean care is the safer choice for winter wear:

    01

    No Shrinking

    No hot wash and no tumble dryer — the two things that shrink wool.

    02

    No Felting

    A gentle solvent cycle keeps fibres from matting into a stiff, felted surface.

    03

    Shape Restored

    Each piece is blocked back to its size, so cuffs, hems and necklines do not flare.

    04

    Softness Kept

    Natural oils in wool are not stripped the way detergent strips them.

    05

    Less Pilling

    Low friction means fewer bobbles on sleeves and sides.

    06

    Stored Folded

    Returned folded flat, because a hanger stretches knitwear out of shape.
