Wash4You's Vyapar Kendra shop — Gate No. 3, Shop No. 180, GF Shushant, Vyapar Kendra Rd, Block C, Sushant Lok Phase I, Sector 43 — is on the Golf Course Road side of Gurugram, which is why this stretch has been part of our regular pickup beat since we started. Our second store at Unitech Arcadia, Sector 49, backs it up. Golf Course Road is not a single neighbourhood so much as a spine of gated high-rises: DLF Aralias, Magnolias and Park Place, Central Park, and the towers and condominiums strung between them. Households here are senior corporate professionals, founders, consultants, expat families and retired couples — people with large wardrobes, long working hours, and very little patience for a garment coming back with a snagged sequin.

What the wardrobes here actually need. Compared with the rest of the city, the mix on this road skews heavily towards things you cannot put in a washing machine. Suits and structured blazers for the office. Silk and chiffon sarees, lehengas and sherwanis with zardozi, mirror-work, sequins and delicate beading that come out for the wedding season. Pashmina, cashmere and merino for the four cold weeks. Evening dresses. Full-length curtains and drapes from double-height living rooms, and fabric sofas that get used hard by kids and dogs. Sneakers too — our before-and-after work on suede uppers like the Nike SB Dunk and cushioned runners like the Hoka Bondi lands well with a household that owns six pairs and wears three. Nazuk kapde, extra care: embellished and hand-finished pieces are inspected, spot-treated and processed separately, never bundled into a general load.

Pickup that works with a gate desk, not against it. Doorstep collection in a tower is a different exercise from a plotted house. Our rider carries the booking name and flat number to the guard desk, waits for the resident or the household staff to approve entry on the visitor app or intercom, uses the service lift where the RWA requires it, and collects at the flat door. If you are at the office, a helper or family member can hand over just as easily, because the count is the safeguard: every order is counted with whoever is at the door, packed separately from every other customer's order, and acknowledged with a written receipt at pickup. Nothing is pooled, nothing is guessed at later. Weekday mornings suit staffed homes; evenings and weekends suit couples who want to hand things over themselves.
