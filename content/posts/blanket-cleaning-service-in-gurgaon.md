## Why Is Regular Blanket Cleaning Important?

Blankets come into close contact with our skin and bedding every day. Over time, dirt and body oils can build up, affecting their freshness and overall condition.

Regular cleaning helps remove accumulated dirt, reduce unpleasant odours, and keep blankets in better condition. It is particularly useful for blankets used throughout the winter or stored away for several months.

## What Types of Blankets Can Be Cleaned?

The appropriate cleaning method depends on the blanket's material, size, construction, and care label.

Common types include:

- Wool blankets
- Cotton blankets
- Fleece blankets
- Polyester blankets
- Microfibre blankets
- Thick winter blankets
- Electric blankets, where the manufacturer's instructions and service capabilities permit cleaning

Electric blankets require special attention because of their electrical components. Never immerse or wash them unless the manufacturer explicitly permits it.

## Professional Blanket Cleaning vs Washing at Home

Washing a blanket at home may be suitable if the care label allows machine washing and your washing machine can accommodate its size and weight.

However, oversized blankets can become very heavy when wet, making them difficult to handle and dry properly. Using unsuitable detergents, excessive heat, or an incorrect washing cycle may also affect the fabric.

Professional cleaning can be helpful for bulky, delicate, or difficult-to-wash blankets. Before booking, check which cleaning method is appropriate for your particular blanket.

## How Often Should You Clean Your Blanket?

Cleaning frequency depends on how often you use the blanket, its material, and whether it becomes visibly dirty.

- Frequently used blankets should be cleaned regularly according to their care labels.
- Blankets affected by spills or noticeable odours may need cleaning sooner.
- Winter blankets should generally be cleaned before long-term seasonal storage.
- Guest-room blankets should be checked and cleaned as needed between uses.

Always allow blankets to dry completely before folding and storing them to help prevent musty smells and moisture-related problems.
