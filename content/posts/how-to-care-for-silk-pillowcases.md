Silk pillowcases feel wonderful against skin and hair, but they need more care than cotton. Washed the wrong way, they lose their softness and sheen. With a few simple habits, a good silk pillowcase can last for years.

## How often to wash

Because it sits against your face and hair every night, a silk pillowcase picks up oils, skincare and sweat. Washing it **every one to two weeks** keeps it fresh. Having a spare means you can rotate them without a gap.

## Hand washing, step by step

1. Fill a clean basin with **cool or lukewarm water**.
2. Add a few drops of **mild, pH-neutral detergent** suitable for silk.
3. Gently swish the pillowcase for a minute or two. Do not rub or twist.
4. Rinse in cool water until all the soap is gone.
5. Press out the water between towels. **Do not wring.**

If you use a machine, put the pillowcase in a mesh bag, choose the gentlest cold cycle and a very low spin. Check the label first, as some silk finishes are dry clean only.

## Drying

Lay it flat on a towel or hang it in the shade. **Keep it out of direct sun and away from the dryer.** Heat and sunlight both weaken silk and fade colour.

## Ironing

If it needs smoothing, iron on the lowest silk setting on the inside while slightly damp, or use a handheld steamer.

## Handling common problems

- **Oil and makeup marks:** blot gently, do not rub, and wash promptly.
- **Dull look:** usually detergent residue. Rinse again in cool water.
- **Snags:** keep jewellery and sharp nails away from the fabric.
- **Colour bleed:** wash new silk alone the first few times.

## Storage

Store clean, dry pillowcases folded in a breathable cotton bag or drawer. Avoid plastic bags that trap moisture.

## Quick checklist

- Wash every one to two weeks
- Cool water, mild detergent, no wringing
- Dry in shade, iron on low
- Store clean and dry, in cotton
