## What Is Steam Ironing?

Steam ironing uses heated steam along with an appropriate ironing temperature to relax fabric fibres and smooth out wrinkles. Compared with dry ironing, steam can make it easier to remove creases from many garments.

The right method depends on the fabric type, garment construction, and care label. Delicate materials such as silk and certain synthetic fabrics may require special settings or alternative finishing methods.

## Steam Ironing vs Regular Ironing

Both steam ironing and dry ironing can help remove wrinkles, but they work differently.

Steam ironing uses moisture to help relax fabric fibres, while dry ironing relies mainly on heat and pressure. Steam can be useful for many cotton and linen garments, whereas certain delicate materials may need dry pressing or another specialised technique.

The best option depends on the fabric, the type of crease, and the manufacturer's care instructions.
