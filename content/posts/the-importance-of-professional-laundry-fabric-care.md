Most people think of laundry as simply washing and drying. But the clothes you wear every day are made of very different fibres, and each one reacts differently to water, heat, detergent and friction. Proper fabric care is what keeps a favourite shirt looking new after fifty washes instead of five.

## Every fabric has its own needs

- **Cotton** is tough but can shrink in hot water.
- **Silk** is delicate and can lose its sheen or stain from water marks.
- **Wool** can felt and shrink when agitated or washed too warm.
- **Synthetics** hold odours and can melt under a hot iron.
- **Embroidered and embellished fabrics** can snag, bleed or lose detail.

The care label is a starting point, but knowing how to follow it, and when to ignore a "machine washable" claim for a valuable piece, takes experience.

## What goes wrong at home

- **Wrong temperature** causes shrinking, fading and colour bleed.
- **Too much detergent** leaves residue that dulls fabric and irritates skin.
- **Overloaded machines** rub clothes against each other and stretch seams.
- **Stains set** when they are rubbed or heat-dried before they are treated.
- **Improper ironing** scorches fibres and leaves shiny patches.

## What professional care does differently

Professionals sort by fabric and colour, test for colour fastness, pre-treat stains and choose the right process for each garment: machine wash, hand wash, wet clean or dry clean. Finishing is done by steam press, which relaxes the fabric without crushing it, and every piece is inspected before it is packed.

## When to dry clean instead of wash

- Suits, blazers and structured jackets
- Silk, velvet and heavily embroidered outfits
- Wedding wear such as sherwanis and lehengas
- Items labelled "dry clean only"
- Garments with delicate linings or trims

## Care that protects your spending

A good suit or saree is an investment. Cleaning it properly and storing it well can add years to its life, which is far cheaper than replacing it. Regular professional care also keeps everyday basics looking fresh for longer.

## Quick checklist

- Read the care label before every wash
- Treat stains early, and never rub them
- Wash dark and light colours separately
- Use less detergent than you think you need
- Hand delicate and expensive items to a professional
