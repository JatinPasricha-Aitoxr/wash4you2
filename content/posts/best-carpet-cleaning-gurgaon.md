## Can All Carpets Be Cleaned the Same Way?

No. Carpets can be made from different materials and may have different construction, colors, patterns, and finishes.

Wool, synthetic, handmade, designer, and delicate carpets can require different cleaning approaches. Using excessive water or unsuitable cleaning products may affect certain carpets.

This is why professional assessment is important before cleaning valuable or delicate carpets.

## How Often Should You Clean Your Carpet?

Cleaning frequency depends on how often the carpet is used and the environment in which it is placed.

Homes with children or pets may require more frequent cleaning. Carpets in busy offices or commercial spaces can also accumulate dirt faster.

Along with regular vacuuming, professional cleaning can help maintain the carpet's overall condition.

## Carpet Stain Removal

Common carpet stains include coffee, tea, food, mud, oil, and beverage spills. The possibility of removing a stain depends on the type of stain, carpet material, and how long the stain has been present.

Avoid aggressively rubbing a fresh stain, as this can sometimes push it deeper into the fibers. Professional assessment can help determine the appropriate treatment.
