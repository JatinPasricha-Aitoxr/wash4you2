## How Often Should You Clean Your Bag?

There is no single cleaning schedule for every bag. The ideal frequency depends on how often you use it, the material, where you carry it, and its overall condition.

A bag used every day may require professional attention more frequently than one used occasionally. You should also consider professional cleaning when you notice visible stains, dirt buildup, unpleasant odors, or changes in the material's appearance.

## Tips to Keep Your Luxury Bag Looking New

Professional **Bag Cleaning Services** can help restore your bag, but everyday care is equally important.

Store your bag in a suitable dust bag when it is not being used.

Keep it away from excessive moisture and direct sunlight.

Avoid placing your bag on dirty or wet surfaces.

Do not overload it, as excessive weight can affect its shape.

Keep makeup, pens, and liquids properly secured inside the bag.

Avoid using household cleaning products without knowing whether they are suitable for the material.

If you notice a difficult stain, contact a professional cleaning service instead of aggressively rubbing it.
