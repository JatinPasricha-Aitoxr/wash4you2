## Tips for Storing a Saree After Dry Cleaning

After professional cleaning, proper storage is also important.

1. Make sure the saree is completely clean and dry before storing it.
2. Fold delicate sarees carefully to reduce unnecessary pressure on embroidery.
3. Store valuable sarees in a clean, dry place.
4. Avoid keeping heavy items directly on top of delicate sarees.
5. Take expensive wedding and designer sarees for professional cleaning when required instead of using harsh home cleaning methods.
