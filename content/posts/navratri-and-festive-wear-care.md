Navratri and the festive season are the perfect occasions to wear beautiful traditional outfits, embroidered lehengas, designer sarees, sherwanis, and elegant ethnic wear. From Garba nights to family gatherings and festive celebrations, these special occasions often involve outfits that require extra care.

However, makeup, sweat, food spills, dust, and prolonged wear can affect the appearance of festive clothing. Proper garment care helps preserve colours, embroidery, fabric quality, and the overall look of your favourite outfits.

If you are preparing your festive wardrobe, these practical tips will help you care for your special garments before, during, and after the celebrations.

## 1. Check the Fabric Before Cleaning

Festive outfits are made from different materials, including silk, velvet, georgette, chiffon, cotton, and synthetic blends. Some garments also feature zari work, sequins, beads, mirrors, or delicate embroidery.

Before washing or cleaning an outfit, check its care label. Avoid assuming that every traditional garment can be washed at home.

For expensive lehengas, silk sarees, designer dresses, and heavily embroidered outfits, professional cleaning may be a safer option when recommended by the manufacturer.

## 2. Treat Stains Carefully

Festive celebrations can lead to accidental stains from food, drinks, cosmetics, and perspiration. Acting promptly can help prevent some stains from becoming more difficult to remove.

Follow these precautions:

- Blot liquid spills gently with a clean, absorbent cloth.
- Avoid rubbing the affected area, as this can spread the stain or damage delicate fibres.
- Do not apply bleach or strong cleaning products without checking fabric compatibility.
- Avoid using heat on a stained area before the stain has been assessed.
- Seek professional help for stubborn stains or delicate garments.

Different stains require different treatments, so avoid experimenting with home remedies on expensive festive clothing.

## 3. Protect Embroidery, Sequins, and Zari Work

Embellished outfits often require special attention because beads, sequins, metallic threads, and decorative stitching can become damaged during unsuitable washing.

Turn garments inside out when appropriate, keep them away from rough surfaces, and avoid placing heavy items on top of delicate embroidery.

For heavily embellished lehengas, designer blouses, and wedding outfits, consult the care label or a professional cleaner before choosing a cleaning method.

## 4. Keep Sarees Fresh After Festive Events

Sarees made from silk, Banarasi fabric, chiffon, georgette, and other delicate materials may need different cleaning and storage methods.

After wearing a saree, allow it to air briefly in a suitable, clean environment before storing it, provided the fabric's care instructions permit this. Check for makeup marks, perspiration, and food stains.

Avoid storing a damp saree or leaving stains untreated for long periods. Fold delicate sarees carefully and use suitable storage materials to help protect them from dust and snagging.

## 5. Care for Lehengas, Anarkalis, and Designer Dresses

Lehengas and Anarkali suits often feature multiple fabric layers, intricate embroidery, and decorative borders. These details can make home washing difficult.

To maintain their appearance:

- Check the garment's care label before cleaning.
- Remove accessories and detachable elements when the instructions allow it.
- Avoid hanging very heavy garments on weak hangers.
- Store outfits in a clean, dry place away from direct sunlight.
- Address stains before putting garments away for long-term storage.

Professional cleaning can help when an outfit requires specialised treatment, but the appropriate method depends on its construction and care instructions.

## 6. Choose the Right Ironing Method

Festive clothing can develop creases during travel, storage, and repeated use. However, applying excessive heat directly to delicate fabrics may cause shine marks, scorching, or damage to embellishments.

Use the ironing method recommended on the care label. Some garments may be suitable for steam ironing, while others require low-temperature pressing or specialised finishing.

Avoid applying a hot iron directly to sequins, beads, metallic embroidery, velvet, or heat-sensitive materials. When in doubt, consult a professional garment-care service.

## 7. Remove Odours Without Damaging Fabric

After a long evening of celebrations, outfits may retain perspiration, perfume, or food odours.

Allow garments to air in a clean, well-ventilated area when appropriate. Avoid spraying excessive perfume or household deodorising products directly onto delicate fabrics, as these may leave marks or affect certain materials.

If an outfit has persistent odours or visible perspiration marks, consider professional cleaning instead of repeatedly applying home treatments.

## 8. Store Festive Outfits Properly After Navratri

Once the celebrations are over, proper storage can help keep your special outfits in good condition for the next occasion.

Follow these simple practices:

- Clean garments according to their care instructions before long-term storage.
- Ensure items are completely dry before putting them away.
- Use clean, breathable storage options suitable for the fabric.
- Keep outfits away from moisture and direct sunlight.
- Avoid overcrowding wardrobes, which can cause creasing and snagging.
- Store heavy lehengas and embellished garments in a way that minimises stress on the fabric and stitching.

Periodically inspect stored garments to check for moisture, odours, or other signs of damage.

## 9. When Should You Choose Professional Dry Cleaning?

Professional cleaning may be appropriate for garments that are difficult to wash at home or require specialised handling.

Consider professional care for:

- Silk and designer sarees
- Embroidered lehengas
- Bridal and wedding outfits
- Velvet and delicate festive dresses
- Sherwanis and formal ethnic wear
- Expensive garments with intricate embellishments

Before booking, explain the garment's material and any stains. Ask about the recommended cleaning method, expected turnaround time, and pricing.
