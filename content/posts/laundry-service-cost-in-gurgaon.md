If you are searching for the laundry service cost in Gurgaon, you are not alone. Prices vary a lot between neighbourhood presses, app-based services and premium cleaners, and the cheapest quote is not always the cheapest outcome. This guide explains how laundry and dry cleaning are priced, what pushes a price up or down, and what you should expect to pay.

## How laundry is priced in Gurgaon

Most services use one of two models:

- **Per kilo** for everyday clothes that can be machine washed, such as T-shirts, bedsheets and towels.
- **Per piece** for garments that need individual care: suits, sarees, lehengas, jackets, shoes and anything delicate.

Wash & fold is the budget option and is billed by weight. Dry cleaning is billed per item, because every garment is treated, finished and inspected on its own.

## What changes the price

- **Fabric:** silk, wool and embroidered fabrics need gentler, slower handling.
- **Embellishment:** beadwork, sequins and zari work add time and risk.
- **Stains:** old or set stains need spot treatment before cleaning.
- **Garment size and layers:** a lined sherwani is more work than a plain shirt.
- **Turnaround:** laundry is usually back in 24 hours; dry cleaning takes 48 to 72 hours.

## Hidden costs to ask about

A low headline rate can hide extras. Before you book, ask:

- Is pickup and delivery free, or charged per order?
- Is GST included or added on top?
- Is there a minimum order value?
- Who is responsible if a garment is damaged?

## Quick checklist before you choose a service

- Compare the price **per item**, not just the headline rate.
- Check what is included: ironing, folding, packaging, delivery.
- Ask how stains and delicate fabrics are handled.
- Look for a clear, published rate card rather than "call for price".
- Read reviews that mention the same garments you own.
