Between long workdays, commutes and family time, laundry is the chore that never quite ends. A professional laundry service takes it off your plate entirely. Here is why more busy households in Gurgaon and across Delhi NCR are making the switch.

## You get your time back

Washing, drying, sorting, folding and ironing can easily take several hours every week. Handing it over frees up weekends for things you would rather be doing. With free doorstep pickup and delivery, you do not even need to leave home.

## Your clothes last longer

Home machines and rushed wash cycles are hard on fabric. Professionals use the right detergent, temperature and cycle for each type of garment, and separate colours and delicates properly. That means less fading, shrinking and stretching, and clothes that keep their shape for longer.

## The right care for every fabric

Not everything belongs in a washing machine. Suits, silk sarees, woollens, leather and embroidered pieces need specific treatment. A professional service can dry clean, hand wash or steam press each piece the way the care label intends, so you are never guessing.

## Consistent, crisp finishing

Steam pressing gives shirts, trousers and sarees a clean, professional finish that is hard to match with a home iron, and it saves you the effort. Everything comes back folded or on hangers, ready for the wardrobe.

## It can be cheaper than you think

When you count detergent, water, electricity, drying and your own time, home laundry is not free. Professional wash and fold starts at just ₹79 per kg, and steam ironing starts at ₹29 for everyday clothes (GST extra). You can see every rate on our [pricing page](/pricing/).

## What to look for in a laundry service

- **Clear pricing,** published up front
- **Reliable pickup and delivery slots**
- **Careful handling,** with every order counted in front of you
- **Gentle, eco-friendly detergents** that are kind to skin and fabric
- **Quick turnaround:** laundry in about 24 hours, dry cleaning in 48 to 72 hours

## Quick checklist for a good first order

- Pick a service that publishes its rates
- Point out stains and delicate items when you hand over
- Check the count of items at pickup and delivery
- Start with a small order to test quality
