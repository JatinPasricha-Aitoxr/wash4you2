The rainy season brings welcome relief from the heat, but it also makes laundry day a struggle. Humid air slows drying, and clothes left damp for even a few hours can pick up a musty smell that is hard to remove. These tips help you dry clothes properly through the monsoon.

## Why clothes smell during the monsoon

That sour, damp smell comes from bacteria and mildew growing in fabric that stays wet too long. The longer a garment takes to dry, the more likely it is to smell, even after a good wash. The goal is simple: get water out fast and get air moving.

## Start with a better wash

- **Spin properly.** A full spin cycle removes far more water than wringing by hand, so clothes start drying much sooner.
- **Do not overload the machine.** Crowded clothes rinse poorly and hold more moisture.
- **Skip heavy softeners** on towels and sportswear; they can trap damp.
- **Wash heavy items on their own.** Jeans, towels and hoodies slow everything else down.

## Drying clothes indoors

- **Use a drying rack.** Spread clothes out so air reaches every surface.
- **Space items apart.** Overlapping fabric stays wet in the folds.
- **Run a fan** pointing at the clothes, or place the rack near a window that stays slightly open.
- **Hang shirts and trousers on hangers** so air moves through them.
- **Turn jeans and thick fabrics inside out** and flip them halfway through the day.
- **Use a dehumidifier or run the AC in dry mode** if you have one, for a closed room.

## What to avoid

- **Folding clothes that feel even slightly damp.** Put them away only when completely dry.
- **Piling wet clothes in a basket** while you wait for a sunny day.
- **Drying in a closed bathroom** without ventilation.
- **Ironing clothes to finish drying them** if the fabric is delicate; it can scorch or leave marks.

## Finishing touches

If a garment still feels slightly damp, a quick pass with a steam iron on a suitable setting dries it fully and refreshes the fabric. Store clothes only when they are completely dry, and keep the wardrobe ventilated with a few sachets or a dry cloth bag to absorb moisture.

## Quick checklist

- Spin cycle on, machine not overloaded
- Rack in the airiest room, with a fan on
- Heavy items dried separately
- Nothing folded or stored until completely dry
- Wardrobe kept ventilated
