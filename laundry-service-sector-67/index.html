    • Large complexes here require gate sign-in and service-lift access, so we build extra time into every slot.

    Working inside a large complex

    A multi-tower complex has rules, and they are there for good reasons. Vendors sign in, use a particular gate, park in a marked bay and take the service lift. All of that adds minutes, and the way to keep a pickup pleasant is to expect them. When you book from Sector 67, tell us the tower and whether your complex needs a resident to approve entry at the gate, and we will call you from the gate rather than standing there arguing with a guard.

    Where a complex will not allow riders past the lobby, we count your order downstairs and hand you the written receipt there. Nothing about the count changes; every piece is still counted in front of you.

    Rugs are the big-flat job

    The flats here are large and most living rooms have a rug that nobody wants to roll up and carry anywhere. That is precisely why they get left for years. We collect carpets and rugs from your door, clean them and return them once they are genuinely dry — and drying is the part that cannot be hurried. A rug returned damp will smell within a week, so if it needs an extra day we keep it an extra day and tell you so.

    Curtains go the same way, booked as a day rather than a slot because the panels have to come down first and be rehung after.

    And the weekly wash

    Underneath the big jobs is the ordinary one: family wash, ironing, dry cleaning, bedding. Our Arcadia counter in Sector 49 is the nearest of our two shops and the run here is short. Pickup and delivery are free. Call or WhatsApp +91 63 67 600 500 with your tower number and what is coming out.
