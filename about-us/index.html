About us

Gurgaon's Most Trusted Laundry & Dry-Cleaning Service

Wash4You is a premium laundry service in Gurugram and Delhi NCR, built on a simple promise: your laundry is our responsibility.

As a unit of Vaaruni Ventures LLP, we combine 25+ years of European garment-care expertise with hydrocarbon dry-cleaning technology to deliver eco-friendly, doorstep laundry and dry-cleaning that saves you time and protects your clothes. Whether you need wash & fold, premium dry cleaning for designer wear, or steam ironing for office formals, our team treats every garment as if it were our own.

Our story

From a handful of clients to Gurugram's favourite laundry partner

What started as a small service for a few local families has grown into one of the most reliable online laundry services in Delhi NCR — entirely through word-of-mouth, consistent quality, and an obsession with customer satisfaction.

We didn't just bring another “dhobi” to the market. We built a technology-enabled laundry ecosystem where you can schedule a pickup online, track your order, and get your clothes back — freshly cleaned, perfectly pressed and neatly packed — without stepping out of your home or office.

Our leadership team brings more than 25 years of hands-on experience from the European laundry and dry-cleaning market, where standards for fabric care, stain removal and eco-friendly processing are among the strictest in the world. We've adapted those practices for local fabrics and climates, and packaged them into an affordable, hassle-free service for professionals, families and businesses across the NCR.

Our mission

Elevating everyday life through better laundry

Laundry shouldn't be a chore. Our mission is to elevate daily life with efficient, eco-friendly, high-quality laundry that gives you back your weekends and your peace of mind. Every wash cycle, every steam press, every doorstep delivery is designed around one question: does this make our customer's life better?

Our vision

We envision a future where every household and workplace in Delhi NCR has access to professional, sustainable garment care — without the hassle of traditional laundry. We are working towards it by continuously investing in:

How it works

From your door and back, in seven steps.

The same care as a specialist store, with the pickup and delivery done for you.

  1. 01

    Book a free pickup

    Choose a time online or on WhatsApp. Pickup and delivery are always free across Gurugram and Delhi NCR.

  2. 02

    Doorstep collection

    We arrive at your slot and count every piece with you before it leaves. No guesswork, no mix-ups.

  3. 03

    Sorting & inspection

    Sorted by fabric and colour; stains, damage and special care are flagged by our fabric experts.

  4. 04

    Stain treatment

    Marks are pre-treated by hand with the right solution for the fabric.

  5. 05

    Cleaning

    Dry-cleaned or washed by the method each garment actually needs — hydrocarbon dry cleaning for delicates and structured wear, water-based washing for the rest.

  6. 06

    Finishing & quality check

    Steam-pressed, re-counted and checked piece by piece against your list before packing.

  7. 07

    Delivered to your door

    Packed separately as your order and returned when we said we would — fresh, crisp, and ready to wear.
